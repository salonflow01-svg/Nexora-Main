# Nexora Studio

**Digital systems for ambitious businesses.**

Nexora Studio is a digital technology studio focused on building modern digital experiences, business systems, and growth infrastructure that help businesses operate smarter, compete stronger, and scale with confidence.

**Live Website:** https://nexoraio.vercel.app/

---

## About

Nexora is built around the idea that a business needs more than just a website.

We connect:

* **Strategy** — Clear direction built around business goals
* **Experience** — Digital experiences and customer journeys designed to build trust and drive action
* **Systems** — Connected tools, workflows, and infrastructure that reduce operational friction
* **Intelligence** — Data, automation, and insights that support better decisions

The goal is to create a connected digital foundation rather than isolated digital assets.

---

## What We Build

Nexora focuses on digital solutions including:

* Business websites
* Digital experiences
* Responsive web interfaces
* Customer journeys
* Business systems
* Workflow solutions
* WhatsApp integrations
* SEO foundations
* Website maintenance
* Digital growth infrastructure

The studio primarily serves service-based businesses, including salons, barbershops, spas, beauty businesses, and other local businesses, while also working with businesses internationally.

---

## Tech Stack

This website is built using a lightweight frontend stack:

* **HTML5**
* **CSS3**
* **Vanilla JavaScript**
* **Vercel**

The project focuses on semantic structure, responsive design, performance, polished UI, and a clear user experience without relying on a heavy frontend framework.

## Design Philosophy

Nexora's visual direction is built around:

* Clean, modern interfaces
* Strong typography
* Clear visual hierarchy
* Generous spacing
* Responsive layouts

* Laptop
